# Flip clock

A face for the screensaver's clock: the hours and the minutes on two flip cards in the screen's largest digits, with
the date and the outside temperature under them, white on black like every screensaver. A dot between the cards blinks
every second; you can turn it off.

It is the smallest screensaver of a plugin and an example to learn from: no pictures, no taps, nothing to fetch. It
works on every screen, the CYD too.

## Set up

1. Add the plugin to a screen in Tessera. The screen builds once.
2. Open the screen's **Settings** and the **Screensaver** card. Turn on **Show in standby** (the screen needs **Auto
   standby** on too).
3. Click the **Clock** step and choose **Flip clock** under **Face**.
4. Choose the temperature under the clock in the same drawer, or none. Turn **Seconds dot** off for a clock that is
   quite still.

## Good to know

- The clock shows the time as the screen writes it, 24 hours or AM and PM, and the date in the screen's language.
- The screen moves the whole clock a millimetre every minute, so nothing stands on the same pixels for hours.
- A screen without the plugin shows Tessera's own clock instead.
