# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version: the newest albums, EPs and singles of up to eight artists you pick, from Deezer, as a cover flow of
  their covers that moves on by itself, with "New" on a release of the last two weeks. Tap left or right to go back or
  on, tap the middle cover to open the release with its cover large and play it on a Music Assistant player. Needs a
  screen with plugin API 0.13. A board without pictures shows a record for each release instead of its cover.
